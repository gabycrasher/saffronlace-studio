    How long do knotless braids take?

    Medium knotless braids to mid-back take about four and a half hours. We'll confirm the time when you book.

    Do I need to wash my hair first?

    No. Every hair service starts with a wash and scalp check at the basin.

    What if I'm running late?

    Message us. Up to 15 minutes we simply start; after that we may need to shorten the style.
