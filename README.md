# Saffronlace Studio: salon website concept

A fictional Kampala hair, braids, nails and skin studio, built as a design showcase.

- **Live:** https://gabycrasher.github.io/saffronlace-studio/
- **Signature opening:** strands of hair fall down the screen and sway, a saffron comb glints across them, and the hair parts down the middle to reveal the studio (about 3 seconds, once per visit, skippable).
- **Highlights:** a service menu where tapping services builds your visit with a live time and price total; a three-question style finder; a draggable lookbook; chair booking with stylist, day and time slots; a gift card designer with a 3D flip; open-now status in Kampala time.
- Static HTML/CSS/JS with GSAP + ScrollTrigger (vendored). Photos from Unsplash (credited in the footer).

The studio, stylists, prices, reviews and address are invented. Nothing is booked, paid or sent. The WhatsApp button contacts the designer.
